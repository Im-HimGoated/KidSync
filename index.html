The details that make a plan useful

More than colored blocks on a calendar.

KidsSync keeps the context behind every commitment close to the schedule, so the next person knows what is happening and what they need to do.

Plan with context
Keep locations, pickup notes, deadlines, and repeating rules attached to the event.
Share with clear access
Owners manage access, editors update plans, and read-only viewers can follow along without changing anything.
Make handoffs easier
Print or export a PDF for grandparents, babysitters, coaches, or school.
Understand the whole week
Compare busy days, workload by child, conflicts, and upcoming deadlines from one dashboard.
