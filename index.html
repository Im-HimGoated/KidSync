The details that make a plan useful

More than colored blocks on a calendar.

KidsSync keeps the context behind every commitment close to the schedule, so the next person knows what is happening and what they need to do.

Plan with context
Keep locations, pickup notes, deadlines, and repeating rules attached to the event.
Share with clear access
Owners manage access, editors update plans, and read-only viewers can follow along without changing anything.
Make handoffs easier
Print or export a PDF for grandparents, babysitters, coaches, or school.
Understand the whole week
Compare busy days, workload by child, conflicts, and upcoming deadlines from one dashboard.

    How KidsSync works

    A few quick ways to move through your family week.

    Click a time

    Click any open hour to start an event there.

    Click a day

    Click a day header or date to open an event with that day already selected.

    Choose a color

    Use the color dots in event details to make important plans easy to spot.

    Add attendees

    Choose All or select the family members connected to an event.

    Move an event

    Drag an event to another day or time. Click it to edit details.

    Share with care

    Sign in to share with viewers, commenters, or editors.

    Use holidays

    Open Holidays to see built-in holidays, then add your own event on the same date.

    Personalize Settings

    Change appearance, time format, default view, event length, and motion preferences in Settings.
